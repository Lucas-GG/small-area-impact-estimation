Ratio of the RMSE of the noncore aggregate cumulative prediction through event year 4 with the screening rule to the RMSE without it; values below 1 mean screening helps. Rule fixed on half A (pre-registered): exclude counties whose standardized held-out error from a backdated refit exceeds 2 in absolute value, each model with its own diagnostic; half B estimates its effect on the other half of the counties.
Half B: Monte Carlo SEs paired by replication in parentheses; R = 200 replications per scenario. Coverage change: change in coverage of the nominal 95% aggregate interval on half B from screening, in percentage points, averaged over scenarios.
Non-TWFE pooled: ETWFE, GB-Ens and RW-IID weighted equally within each scenario. RW-IID is the nested model (default priors); GB-Ens has the ETWFE baseline. Halves A and B share year shocks within a replication, so half-B SEs somewhat overstate precision.
