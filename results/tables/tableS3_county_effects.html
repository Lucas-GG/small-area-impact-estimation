County-level accuracy of the estimated cumulative effect through event year 4, noncore pseudo-treated counties; every true county effect is zero. Metrics are computed within each replication over the same counties for all models and averaged over 200 replications; marginal Monte Carlo SEs in parentheses.
County-effect RMSE: root mean squared county effect (root-PEHE). Centered RMSE: the same after removing each replication's mean estimated effect, i.e. spurious between-county differences net of the aggregate error. County CRPS: continuous ranked probability score of each county's predictive distribution at the true effect (lower is better). Coverage and Width: coverage and mean width of county 95% intervals. Smaller dispersion is desirable here only because the true heterogeneity is zero.
Mean pseudo-treated counties per replication: MAR 34.4; Latent level 54.0; Latent trend 49.4. Noise floor of the county-effect RMSE (known expected counts): MAR 1.33; Latent level 1.32; Latent trend 1.23.
DGP: data-generating process of the placebo study. RW-IID and RW-ST are the nested models (default priors); GB-Ens has the ETWFE baseline.
